1. Phishing Simulation
**Difficulty:** Very Easy  
**Category:** Social Engineering  

**Objective:** Analyze a suspicious email and identify the phishing indicator.

**Scenario:**
You received an email from `admin@schoolupdates.com` claiming you need to verify your school password. Open the email and find signs of phishing.

**Instructions:**
- Review the provided email file `email.eml` (certain readers may not allow you to see the flag).
- Identify the spoofed link.
- Find the hidden flag embedded in the link.

**Hint:** Hover over the link and observe where it really leads.

**Flag Format:** `FLAG{secret_message}`
